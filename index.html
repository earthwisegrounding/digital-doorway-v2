01 The situation

You do good work. The internet has no idea.

A neighbor asks who fixed your sink, who did the cake, who handled the closing. Your name comes up. Then they do what everyone does — they look you up on their phone while standing in their kitchen.

And they find a Facebook page you last posted to in 2021, a phone number that may or may not be current, and three competitors with real websites sitting above you. You didn’t lose that job on price. You lost it in the time it took them to thumb past you.

That’s the whole problem. Not a rebrand. Not a retainer. One page that says what you do, where you do it, and how to reach you.

04 The week

Seven days, four conversations, then it’s live.

  1. Day 1

    One phone call

    Thirty minutes. What you do, who calls you, what they ask first, and what you wish they knew. Bring photos if you have them; if you don’t, we’ll work around it.

  2. Day 2–3

    We write it and build it

    You get a real link on a real screen, not a PDF mock-up. Words, layout, photos, phone number — the whole thing, working, on your own phone.

  3. Day 4–6

    You mark it up

    Two rounds. Say “that’s not how we say it” as many times as you need to. Nobody here is precious about a sentence.

  4. Day 7

    Live, and yours

    Domain pointed, hosting in your name, forms tested, logins emailed to you. We check back a week later to make sure the messages are actually landing.

05 Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

  1. The catch
  2. Ownership
  3. Not a computer person
  4. Changes later
  5. Google
What’s the catch at $499?
There isn’t one, there’s a limit. Five pages, two rounds of changes, one build. We’re fast because we’ve done this shape of site many times over. If your job is genuinely bigger — booking systems, online ordering, a hundred products — we’ll say so on the call and quote it properly or point you elsewhere.
Do I actually own it?
Yes. The domain is registered to you, the hosting account is in your name, and the files are yours to take anywhere. Nothing is held hostage. If you fire us tomorrow the site keeps running.
I’m not a computer person.
Good — that’s the normal case. You won’t be asked to pick a template, learn a builder, or write anything. You talk, we type. The only technical thing you’ll do is forward us a photo or two.
What if I need a change in six months?
Email us. Small edits are usually free because they take four minutes. Anything real is $75 an hour, quoted first. Or we’ll show you how to change your own hours and prices — it takes twenty minutes to learn.
Will it show up on Google?
You’ll be set up correctly: fast pages, proper titles, your service area and hours marked up so Google can read them, and your Business Profile tidied and linked. That’s the foundation, and for most local trades it’s the bulk of it. Anyone promising you the top spot for a competitive term is guessing.

And the part we put in writing

You see the first version before you pay a penny. If it isn’t right, you don’t pay, and you owe us nothing.
