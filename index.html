01 The situation

You do good work. The internet has no idea.

A neighbour asks who fixed your sink, who did the cake, who handled the closing. Your name comes up. Then they do what everyone does — they look you up on their phone while standing in their kitchen.

And they find a Facebook page you last posted to in 2021, a phone number that may or may not be current, and three competitors with real websites sitting above you. You didn’t lose that job on price. You lost it in eleven seconds of scrolling.

That’s the whole problem, and it’s a solvable one. Not with a rebrand. Not with a monthly retainer. With one clear, fast, honest page that answers what you do, where you do it, and how to reach you — and then gets out of the way.

04 How the week goes

Seven days, four conversations, then it’s live.

  1. Day 1

    A phone call, thirty minutes

    What you do, who calls you, what they ask first, what you wish they knew. Bring photos if you have them. If you don’t, we’ll work around it — no stock handshakes.

  2. Day 2–3

    We write it and design it

    You get a real link on a real screen, not a PDF mockup. Words, layout, photos, phone number — the whole thing, working, on your phone.

  3. Day 4–6

    You mark it up, we fix it

    Two rounds. Say “that’s not how we say it” as many times as you need to. This is the part most people are surprised to enjoy.

  4. Day 7

    Live, and handed over

    Domain pointed, hosting in your name, forms tested, logins emailed to you. We check it a week later to make sure the leads are actually landing.

05 Straight answers

The questions people ask before they trust us.

What’s the catch at $499?
There isn’t one, there’s a limit. Five pages, two rounds of changes, one build. We’re fast because we’ve done this shape of site many times over. If your job is genuinely bigger — booking systems, online ordering, a hundred products — we’ll say so on the call and quote it properly or point you elsewhere.
Do I actually own it?
Yes. The domain is registered to you, the hosting account is in your name, and the files are yours to take anywhere. Nothing is held hostage. If you fire us tomorrow the site keeps running.
I’m not a computer person.
Good — that’s the normal case. You won’t be asked to pick a template, learn a builder, or write anything. You talk, we type. The only technical thing you’ll do is forward us a photo or two.
What if I need a change in six months?
Email us. Small edits are usually free because they take four minutes. Anything real is $75 an hour, quoted first. Or we’ll show you how to change your own hours and prices — it takes twenty minutes to learn.
Will it show up on Google?
You’ll be set up correctly: fast pages, proper titles, your service area and hours marked up so Google can read them, and your Business Profile tidied and linked. That’s the foundation, and for most local trades it’s the bulk of it. Anyone promising you the top spot for a competitive term is guessing.
